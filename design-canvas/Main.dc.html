One slab, nothing hanging off

Built-in battery, Bluetooth to three devices or USB-C. Pick it up and type on the sofa, the desk or your lap.

Modular underneath

Two flush 4 mm bays in the base take an extra battery, a 2.4 GHz receiver, a security key or your own board. It works with both empty.

A palm rest for your hands

The front panel swaps: standard trackpad, a big-hands version with a lower trackpad and a thumb ridge, or a plain wrist rest.

Made for sweaty hands too

Glass or soft-touch polymer palm rests that sweat can't corrode, PBT keycaps, and a sealed layer under the keys.

Compute lives elsewhere

The Spine is a separate Mac mini-size box on one USB-C cable, with the board, storage and ports.
