Deck One · the slab and the palm rest

One flat slab. One optional swap.

Deck One is a keyboard and trackpad with a battery and a cable. The only part made to swap is the palm rest, because that's where hands differ and where wear happens.

The slab

Side profile · thickness drawn 4×

221 mm front to back · 5 mm thick, flat · about an iPhone Fold opened out

Battery and cable

Two flat cells under the palm rest, either side of the trackpad. USB-C on the back edge for charging and wired use; Bluetooth to three devices.

Open firmware

ZMK: remap any key, set the trackpad pause after typing, lock the trackpad with fn + esc.

Optional: pick the palm rest for your hands · 1.3 px per mm

Standard

140 × 90 mm trackpad, 5.3 mm below the keys. Most room for gestures.

Big hands

120 × 76 mm trackpad set 18.6 mm below the keys, behind a 1 mm thumb ridge, so palms and thumbs don't land on it while typing.

Wrist rest

No trackpad at all. For a mouse, a pen tablet, or anyone who never wants an accidental click.

Palm-rest materials

Aluminium. Hard-anodised and sealed. The default; matches the frame.

Glass. One sheet across the whole palm rest, trackpad included. Sweat can't corrode it and it wipes clean.

Soft-touch polymer. No bare metal under your wrists, warmer to rest on, and a cheap part to replace when it wears.

Built for big and sweaty hands

  • Trackpad ignores touches for a moment after each keystroke, and rejects resting palms.
  • fn + esc locks the trackpad completely, with a light to show it's off.
  • PBT keycaps that don't go shiny, over a sealed layer that keeps sweat off the switches.
  • Coated circuit board, and no bare metal where skin touches if you choose glass or polymer.
  • Palm rest is a separate part: replace it, not the keyboard.
