Deck One · modules, profile and spec

One deck, three spines

The deck itself is just keys, trackpad and a magnetic connector along the back edge. Whatever clips on there decides how it connects and how it's powered.

In the box

Battery spine

status light · host button · USB-C

14 mm deep. Battery plus Bluetooth for up to three hosts: your Mac mini, an iPad, a Pi. Charge it over USB-C and keep typing while it charges.

Lightest

Cable spine

USB-C to host · USB-C pass-through

10 mm deep. No battery, no radio, nothing to charge. One cable to the Mac mini, with a spare port for a drive or a security key.

For builders

Compute spine

swappable I/O plate, cut to match the board

66 mm deep. A bay for an 85 × 56 mm board (Raspberry Pi-size and similar SoC boards) on standard mounting holes. The deck talks to it over internal USB; plug in a screen and it's a computer.
