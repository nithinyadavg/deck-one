# The swappable palm rest

Deck One works as one fixed slab. If you want to, you can swap one part: the **palm-rest top plate**, the front 99 mm of the top surface with the trackpad in it. It's held by two latches underneath and connects to the trackpad controller through a small connector at the seam. The base and the battery cells under the palm rest stay in place.

Why this part, and only this part:

- **Hands differ.** One trackpad position can't suit everyone.
- **It takes the most wear.** The palm rest gets sweat, oils and scratches, and should be cheap to replace without replacing the keyboard.
- **Materials can change** without touching anything else.

## Materials

| Material | Why |
|---|---|
| **Aluminium** (default) | Matches the base. Hard-anodised and sealed to resist sweat, but still metal under the wrists. |
| **Glass** | One sheet across the whole palm rest, trackpad included. Doesn't react with sweat, doesn't stain, wipes clean. |
| **Soft-touch polymer** | No metal contact at all, warmer to rest on, and the cheapest plate to replace when it wears. |

## Built for big and sweaty hands

Accidental trackpad touches and sweat damage are two of the most common complaints about laptop-style keyboards, especially for people with large hands or very sweaty hands (hyperhidrosis).

**Accidental touches**
- Big-hands plate: the trackpad sits 18.6 mm below the keys (vs 5.3 mm), behind a 1 mm thumb ridge you can feel.
- Typing-aware firmware: the trackpad ignores touches for a short moment after each keystroke (default 250 ms, adjustable).
- Palm rejection on large contact areas.
- **fn + esc** locks the trackpad completely, with a small light to show it's off.
- Or skip the trackpad entirely with the wrist-rest plate.

**Sweat**
- Glass or soft-touch polymer palm rests: no bare metal under the wrists.
- PBT keycaps, which don't go shiny the way ABS caps do.
- A sealed membrane layer under the keys keeps moisture off the switches.
- Conformal coating on the circuit board.
- The palm rest is a replaceable plate, so wear doesn't mean a new keyboard.

## Making your own plate

The seam is a straight line 122 mm from the back edge. Any plate 304 mm wide and 99 mm deep that fits the 5 mm slab and uses the two latch points can be a palm rest. The top views in [`../drawings`](../drawings) show the seam line.
